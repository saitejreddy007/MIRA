Relationship Memory
Not storing data. Making the client feel known.
▾
Layered Memory Architecture
Core Layer
Never expires. Always active. Payment personality, relationship coordinate, key promises made, major disputes, what works with this client, communication preferences, how long they've been a client.
Recent Layer
Last 30 days. Last 5 interactions verbatim. Recent emotional coordinate readings. Recent behavioral signals. Most current relationship temperature. Always fed into generation.
Episodic Layer
Everything older than 30 days compressed into structured summaries. "October — client was going through cash flow difficulty. Responded well to instalment offer. Paid in two parts." Retrieved selectively by relevance.
Human Detail Layer
Never expires. Product launches mentioned. Team expansions shared. Difficult periods referenced. Personal context offered naturally. These are what make Priya feel like a real person who genuinely knows this client.

Eight Reply Types — Classification Engine
Payment Confirmation
"Just sent the transfer."
Verify. Warm acknowledgment. Relationship strengthening close.
Promise to Pay
"Will sort this by Friday."
Graceful acceptance. Specific follow-up scheduled. Promise recorded in memory.
Partial Payment Signal
"Can I pay half now?"
Intelligent negotiation within business parameters. Options presented.
Dispute Signal
"I don't think this amount is right."
Immediate de-escalation. Door fully opened. Human flagged with full context.
Delay Request
"Going through difficult time."
Empathy first. Zero pressure. Flexibility offered within parameters.
Deflection
"I'll check with accounts team."
Patience. Specific follow-up. Gentle accountability. Subtext read for real meaning.
Aggression
"Stop sending me these emails."
Immediate de-escalation. Tone shifts completely. Human flagged if continues.
Ghost Breaking
Responds after long silence.
Warm re-engagement. No reference to silence. Fresh start energy completely.
